# GUZA GURA — Product Constitution

Status: MVP scaffold. Launch market: Mbarara, Uganda; expand across Western Uganda. Currency: UGX. Platform: mobile-first installable PWA.

## Users and problem
Buyers discover local offers; individuals and merchants list goods; moderators review listings and reports. Local inventory is fragmented across shops and social channels. The product provides searchable local discovery, clear price/condition, seller profiles and direct enquiries.

## Scope
MVP: listings, search and filters, profiles, saved listings, enquiries, moderation and checkout foundations. Excludes warehousing, guaranteed delivery, escrow, lending, cross-border logistics and native mobile apps.

## Transactions
Classified buyers negotiate directly and arrange payment/collection; platform does not hold funds. Eligible merchant checkout uses server-validated order/inventory flows. Cash on collection is not proof of payment. Digital payments settle only after verified server-side provider confirmation.

## Categories
Phones/tablets, computers, fashion, home/furniture, food/agriculture, vehicles, baby/kids and services. Property/jobs need additional moderation rules.

## Revenue
Start with free listings; later promoted listings, merchant subscriptions, disclosed checkout commissions and sponsored placements.

## Trust and metrics
Moderation, reporting, safe-meetup guidance, prohibited-item rules, rate limits and payment audit trails. Track active sellers, published listings, search-to-view, enquiries, response time, checkout conversion, cancellations, reports and repeat use.

## Launch gates
Test RLS, storage, authorization, payment callbacks/idempotency, backup restore; publish privacy, terms, prohibited-items, refund and complaints policies. Keep classified and checkout flows separate. Do not custody customer funds until compliant provider and operating model are validated.
